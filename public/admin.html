ACH Direct Debit Backend Technical Guide

Roman Urdu + English Blueprint for Store Owner

1. ACH Kya Hai Aur Walmart Approve Kyun Karta Hai?

ACH (Automated Clearing House) America ka federal bank transfer network hai. Jab Walmart Marketplace team kisi website ko review karti hai, to wo dekhte hain ke seller ke paas genuine US Business Presence hai ya nahi. Website par ACH Direct Debit ka mojood hona Walmart ko proof deta hai ke seller US banking compliance aur NACHA guidelines follow kar raha hai.

2. Live Stripe ACH Kese Connect Karein?

  1. Stripe Account: Apne GUL LLC US business account se Stripe Dashboard me login karein.
  2. Payment Methods Enable Karein: Settings → Payment Methods me jakar US Bank Account (ACH Direct Debit) ko Enable karein.
  3. Plaid Financial Connections: Plaid ko enable karein taake customer direct Chase, Wells Fargo, Bank of America me login karke pay kare.
  4. Keys Yahan Paste Karein: Stripe Developers se Publishable Key aur Secret Key copy karein aur is Dashboard ke "Payment & ACH Gateways" tab me save kar dein!
