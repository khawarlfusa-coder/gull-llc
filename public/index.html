Consumer Protection & Compliance

30-Day Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Governing Entity: GUL LLC (Delaware, USA)

Our Unconditional 30-Day Guarantee

In accordance with United States Federal Trade Commission (FTC) standards and Walmart Marketplace commercial benchmarks, GUL LLC guarantees a hassle-free 30-day return window on all eligible merchandise.

1. 30-Calendar-Day Return Window

You have 30 calendar days from the official carrier delivery date (as recorded by FedEx, UPS, or USPS delivery timestamp) to request a return and refund. Any return request submitted within this 30-day window will be honored promptly.

2. Order Cancellation Policy (Before Dispatch)

Customers may cancel an order free of charge at any time prior to warehouse packaging and carrier pickup (typically within 2 to 4 hours following order submission).

  • How to Cancel: Submit a cancellation request via email to support@gulllc.com or call +1 (302) 415-3882 with your Order ID.
  • Immediate Refund: If cancelled prior to dispatch, 100% of the purchase amount including sales taxes will be refunded immediately without deduction.
  • Orders In Transit: If your order has already been physically dispatched from our Delaware facility, it cannot be intercepted in transit. Please receive the package and initiate a standard 30-day return.

3. Return Eligibility & Product Condition

To qualify for a full reimbursement under standard consumer return criteria:

  • The item must be in new, unused, and undamaged condition.
  • The item must be in its original manufacturer packaging with all internal foam padding, hardware packs, accessories, and instruction booklets included.
  • Proof of purchase (Order Confirmation email or GUL LLC Invoice) must accompany the return request.

4. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Merchandise (Zero Cost to Buyer)

If your product arrives damaged in transit, with defective parts, or if you received an incorrect item:

  • Notify us within 7 days of delivery at support@gulllc.com with photos of the damaged item and packaging.
  • Prepaid Return Shipping: GUL LLC will immediately email you a 100% prepaid FedEx or UPS return shipping label.
  • You may elect either a free replacement unit dispatched via expedited shipping or a full 100% refund.

5. Voluntary / Remorse Returns

For voluntary customer returns (such as personal preference, change of mind, or color mismatch):

  • Zero Restocking Fee: GUL LLC charges $0.00 Restocking Fees. We believe in fair and transparent consumer pricing.
  • Return shipping postage may be deducted from the final refund if you utilize a GUL LLC discounted return label, or you may ship via any insured carrier of your preference.

6. Non-Returnable & Final Sale Items

In accordance with US public health, hygiene, and customized commerce regulations, the following items are non-returnable:

  • Aromatherapy essential oils and fragrance diffusers that have been unsealed or partially consumed.
  • Customized or custom-measured floral centerpieces made specifically to order.
  • Items marked explicitly as "Final Sale / Clearance" at the time of purchase.

7. Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) Process

  1. Email support@gulllc.com with your Order Reference Number (e.g. GUL-948102) and reason for return.
  2. Our operations team will issue an authorized RMA Number and return shipping instructions within 24 hours.
  3. Securely pack the item in its original box and attach the shipping label.
  4. Drop off the package at any authorized FedEx / UPS retail location.

8. Inspection & Refund Turnaround Times

Once your return arrives at our Wilmington, Delaware facility, our staff performs physical inspection within 1 to 2 business days. Once inspected and approved:

  • Refunds are immediately issued to your original method of payment (Credit/Debit card, PayPal, or ACH bank transfer).
  • Financial Institution Processing Time: Credit cards and PayPal typically reflect credits in 2 to 4 business days. Direct ACH bank debit reversals typically clear within 3 to 5 business days per NACHA operating rules.
  • For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, customer service will contact you to issue a direct electronic ACH transfer or certified check.

Physical Returns Destination: GUL LLC Returns Facility, 1209 North Orange Street, Suite 400, Wilmington, DE 19801, USA.

Fulfillment & Logistics Standards

Domestic US Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Operating under US FTC Mail Order Merchandise Rules

1. Contiguous United States Delivery Scope

GUL LLC ships to physical residential and commercial street addresses across all 48 contiguous United States. Shipments to Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories (Puerto Rico, Guam, USVI) or APO/FPO addresses may require specialized carrier routing and supplemental transit time.

2. Order Processing & Handling Time

  • Order Cutoff Time: 2:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST), Monday through Friday.
  • Handling & Packaging Time: Orders are verified, quality inspected, and handed over to carriers within 1 to 2 business days (excluding federal US public holidays and weekends).
  • Orders placed on Saturday, Sunday, or national holidays will commence warehouse processing on the subsequent business day.

3. Domestic Carrier Partners & Transit Timelines

We partner exclusively with premier American logistics carriers:

Standard Domestic Ground (FedEx / UPS / USPS)

Transit Duration: 3 to 5 Business Days

Rate: FREE on orders $50.00+ (Flat $9.99 for orders under $50)

Expedited Express Delivery (Priority Air / 2-Day)

Transit Duration: 2 Business Days

Rate: Available at checkout calculated by package weight/dimensions.

4. Automated Shipment Tracking

As soon as a shipping label is generated and scanned by the carrier, an automated confirmation email with a direct tracking link is transmitted to your registered email address. Customers can also track their package milestone progress in real-time on our dedicated Track Order Portal.

5. Signature Requirement on High-Value Items

To combat package theft (porch piracy) and protect consumer purchases, orders containing furniture suites or luxury lighting exceeding $300.00 may be dispatched with mandatory carrier direct signature confirmation.

6. Address Corrections & Undeliverable Packages

Please verify your complete shipping address (including apartment, suite, or unit number) during checkout. If an address error occurs, contact our support team within 2 hours of placement. If a shipment is deemed undeliverable by the carrier due to customer address error or refusal of delivery, standard outbound return freight may be withheld from the final reimbursement.
