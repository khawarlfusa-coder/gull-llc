30-Day Refund and Returns Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Governing Entity: GUL LLC

1. 30-Day Return Window

At GUL LLC, our customer satisfaction is paramount. You are entitled to request a return within 30 calendar days of receiving your shipment.

2. Eligibility & Product Condition

To qualify for a full refund, items must be in their original unused condition, including all original packaging, hardware accessories, instruction manuals, and proof of purchase.

3. Pre-Paid Return Labels

If your item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect, GUL LLC will provide a complimentary prepaid FedEx/UPS return label. For buyer remorse or voluntary exchanges, return postage may be deducted from the final refund.

4. Refund Processing Time

Once received at our Delaware fulfillment hub, inspection takes 1-2 business days. Approved refunds are credited back to your original payment method (ACH, PayPal, or 2Checkout Card) within 3-5 business days.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | GUL LLC Fulfillment Network

1. Domestic US Coverage

GUL LLC ships to all 48 contiguous United States. We partner with Tier-1 carriers including FedEx, UPS, and the United States Postal Service (USPS).

2. Processing & Transit Times

  • Order Processing & Packaging: 1 - 2 business days (Monday through Friday).
  • Standard Carrier Transit: 3 - 5 business days nationwide.
  • Expedited Delivery: 2 business days upon request at checkout.

3. Shipping Rates & Free Shipping

We provide FREE Standard Domestic Shipping on all retail orders exceeding $50.00. For orders under $50.00, a flat carrier fee of $9.99 applies.

4. Live Milestone Tracking

Immediately upon dispatch, you will receive an automated tracking code. You can verify real-time package progress directly on our Track Order Portal.
